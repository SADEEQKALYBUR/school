📝 Admission Questions
How do I apply for admission? +
You can apply online through our website by visiting the Apply Now page. Fill out the application form with accurate information about the student and parent/guardian. You can also visit the school in person during working hours to collect and submit a physical application form.
What is the age requirement for each class? +
Playgroup: 2-3 years | Nursery 1: 3-4 years | Nursery 2: 4-5 years | Primary 1: 5-6 years | JSS 1: 11-12 years | SSS 1: 14-15 years. Age requirements may be flexible based on the child's academic ability.
What documents are required for admission? +
Required documents include: Birth Certificate, 4 Passport Photographs, Previous School Report Card (if applicable), Medical/Health Certificate, Parent/Guardian ID Card, Proof of Residence, and Completed Application Form.
Is there an entrance examination? +
Yes, students applying for Primary 1 and above are required to sit for a placement test. This helps us determine the appropriate class level for the student. The test covers basic literacy and numeracy skills. Playgroup and Nursery students do not require an entrance exam.
💰 Fees & Payment Questions
How much are the school fees? +
School fees vary by level. Please visit our Admission page for the complete fee structure. Fees are charged per term and include tuition, exam fees, and other charges. Contact the school bursary for the most current fee schedule.
Can I pay school fees online? +
Yes! You can pay school fees online through our Student Portal. We accept payments via Paystack which supports debit cards, bank transfers, and USSD. You will receive an automatic receipt after successful payment.
Is there a sibling discount? +
Yes, we offer a 10% discount on school fees for the second child and 15% for the third child and beyond from the same family. Please contact the school administration for more details on our sibling discount policy.
📚 Academic Questions
How can I check my child's results? +
You can check results in two ways: (1) Visit our Result Checker page on the website and enter the student's admission number and select the session and term. (2) Login to the Student Portal using the admission number and parent phone number as password.
How many terms are in an academic session? +
There are three terms in each academic session: First Term (September - December), Second Term (January - April), and Third Term (April - July). Each term has continuous assessment tests and a final examination.
What is the grading system? +
Our grading system is: A (70-100) = Excellent, B (60-69) = Very Good, C (50-59) = Good, D (45-49) = Pass, E (40-44) = Weak Pass, F (0-39) = Fail. Each subject is scored out of 100 with 40 marks for Continuous Assessment and 60 marks for the final examination.
💻 Student Portal Questions
How do I login to the Student Portal? +
To login to the Student Portal, visit the Student Portal page and enter your Admission Number as the username and your Parent's Phone Number as the password. If you have trouble logging in, please contact the school administration.
What can I do on the Student Portal? +
On the Student Portal, you can: View and print academic results by term and session, Check school fees payment status and history, Make online payments via Paystack, View and print your Student ID Card, and Monitor attendance records.
❓ General Questions
What are the school hours? +
School hours are Monday to Friday: 7:30 AM - 4:00 PM. The school office is open Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM. Students should arrive by 7:30 AM for morning assembly.
Does the school have a uniform? +
Yes, all students are required to wear the school uniform. The uniform can be purchased from the school store or approved vendors. School uniform consists of a white shirt/blouse, navy blue trousers/skirt, and black shoes.
How do I contact the school? +
You can contact us via phone at +234 800 000 0000, email at info@syms.edu.ng, or visit us at our address in Kano State, Nigeria. You can also fill out the contact form on our Contact Us page and we will respond within 24 hours.
