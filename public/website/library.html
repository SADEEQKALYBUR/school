Library Rules

Library Rules & Hours

1

Maintain Silence

The library is a quiet zone. Students must maintain silence at all times to ensure a conducive learning environment.

2

Handle Books Carefully

Books must be handled with care. No writing, tearing, or defacing of library materials is allowed.

3

Return Books on Time

Borrowed books must be returned within 2 weeks. Late returns will attract a fine of ₦50 per day.

4

No Food or Drinks

Eating and drinking are strictly prohibited inside the library to protect the books and materials.
