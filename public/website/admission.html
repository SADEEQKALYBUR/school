How to Apply

Admission Process

1

Online Application

Fill out the online application form with accurate information about the student and parent/guardian.

2

Document Submission

Submit required documents including birth certificate, passport photos and previous school results.

3

Entrance Assessment

Students sit for a placement test to determine the appropriate class level for enrollment.

4

Payment of Fees

Upon acceptance, pay the required school fees online or at the school bursary.

5

Enrollment Complete

Receive your admission letter, student ID and portal login credentials to begin your journey.
