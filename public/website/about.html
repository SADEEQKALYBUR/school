Our History

A Legacy of Excellence Since 2009

Sani Yahaya Memorial School was founded in 2009 with a vision to provide quality education to the children of Kano and beyond. Named in honor of a great educationist, the school has grown from a small institution to one of the most respected schools in the region.

Over the years, we have produced thousands of successful graduates who are making positive impacts in various fields including medicine, law, engineering, business and public service.
